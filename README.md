# 🧠 Mental Health Awareness Platform

**Promoting Mental Well-being Through Education and Interactive Resources**

A comprehensive React-based mental health platform designed to offer valuable insights on various mental health topics, combined with interactive and engaging resources to promote mental well-being, education, and support for individuals seeking to understand and improve their mental health.

## 🌟 Mission

Mental health affects everyone, yet many struggle to find accessible, reliable, and engaging information about mental well-being. Our platform aims to break down barriers by providing comprehensive education on mental health topics in an approachable and interactive format. We believe that knowledge is power, and by equipping individuals with the right information, we can promote better mental health outcomes and reduce stigma around mental health issues.

## 🏗️ What It Does

### Education Section

The platform provides comprehensive information on mental health through carefully structured content:

**Mental Health Fundamentals:**
- Defines mental health as emotional, psychological, and social well-being
- Explains how mental health affects thinking, feeling, and behavior
- Emphasizes that good mental health is more than absence of illness
- Discusses the importance of resilience, relationships, and coping mechanisms

**Impact on Daily Life:**
- How mental health influences stress management
- Effects on relationships and decision-making
- Connection between mental and physical health
- Role in productivity and life satisfaction

**Helping Others:**
- Active listening and providing non-judgmental support
- Encouraging professional help
- Practical ways to assist someone in need
- Recognizing crisis situations

**Self-Care Guidance:**
- Steps to take when struggling with mental health
- Importance of professional support
- Healthy lifestyle habits (exercise, nutrition, sleep)
- Mindfulness and stress management techniques

**Mental Health Disorders:**
- Overview of common conditions (anxiety, depression, bipolar)
- Personality disorders and their characteristics
- Psychotic disorders like schizophrenia
- Eating disorders and body image issues
- Importance of early diagnosis and treatment

## 🛠️ Installation & Setup

### Prerequisites

- Node.js (v14 or higher)
- npm or yarn
- Git

### Setup Steps

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/yourusername/mental-health-platform.git
   cd mental-health-platform
   ```

2. **Install dependencies:**
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Add images:**
   - Place image files in `/public/images/` directory
   - Ensure the following images are present:
     - `img-11.png` (Mental Health)
     - `img-12.jpg` (Importance)
     - `img-13.jpg` (Helping Others)
     - `img-14.jpg` (Self Help)
     - `img-15.jpg` (Disorders)

4. **Start the development server:**
   ```bash
   npm start
   ```
   
   The app will open at `http://localhost:3000`

5. **Build for production:**
   ```bash
   npm run build
   ```
